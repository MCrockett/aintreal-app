## Full Description

Can you spot the fake? In AIn't Real, you'll see two images side-by-side. One is a real photograph, the other is AI-generated. Your mission: figure out which one AIn't Real!

🎮 THREE GAME MODES

• Party Mode (2-8 players) — Play with friends! Everyone guesses at once. Earn bonus points for speed, streaks, and lucky guesses. Perfect for game nights!

• Classic Solo — Practice at your own pace. Sharpen your AI detection skills with no pressure.

• Marathon — The ultimate challenge. 26 rounds, but one wrong answer ends your run. How far can you go?

⚡ FAST-PACED FUN

Each round gives you just seconds to decide. Trust your instincts. Is that sunset a bit too perfect? Does that smile look slightly off? The clock is ticking!

🏆 COMPETE & CLIMB

• Speed bonuses reward quick thinking
• Streak bonuses for consecutive correct answers
• Random bonuses keep every game exciting
• See how you stack up against friends

🎯 POWERED BY REAL AI

Our image pairs feature genuine stock photos alongside AI-generated recreations. As AI gets better, can you keep up?

• Beautiful dark theme
• Sound effects and haptic feedback
